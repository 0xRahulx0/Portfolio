Professional Summary

Final-year B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science with hands-on experience in data analysis, visualization, and machine learning through academic projects and virtual internships. Proficient in Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI, and Tableau, with a strong foundation in statistical analysis and data-driven decision-making.


My analytical approach starts with the business question, not the tool. I like sitting with messy, real-world datasets — cleaning them, exploring them, and shaping them into dashboards and narratives that make trade-offs, risks, and opportunities obvious to non-technical stakeholders. Whether it’s a Power BI dashboard, a SQL pipeline, or an EDA notebook, the goal is the same: turn raw data into a decision someone can act on tomorrow.


Right now I’m deepening my work across Python, SQL, and BI tooling through project-based learning — building end-to-end analyses on retail, HR, fintech, and fraud-detection datasets — while sharpening the storytelling and communication side of the craft. Alongside that, virtual internships with Deloitte, Quantium, and Tata have given me structured exposure to how analytics actually shows up in a client and product setting.


I’m actively looking for entry-level Data Analyst roles or internships on teams that value curiosity, ownership, and clean thinking as much as clean code — places where I can contribute early, learn fast, and help teams see their data more clearly.
